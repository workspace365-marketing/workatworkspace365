Our story

It started with a mission to simplify everything.

How we started

Work should be simple. Technology should help.

Too often it does the opposite. We started Workspace 365 to change that.

our mission

Everything simplified.

We make work simple, so people can focus on their work.

Think of the apps, documents, devices and passwords that fill a working day. Erik and Hans, two people from Spakenburg, a village in the Netherlands, saw what that does to people. They lose focus. Collaborating gets harder. Work becomes less fun. That's down to the tools.

They knew software could be simpler. In 2010 they started New Day at Work to show it. In 2013 it became Workspace 365.

Today we work from our office in Nijkerk. Our product has grown into an intelligent workspace, and Kelly, our digital colleague, helps people find answers faster. The question we started with has not changed. How do we make work simpler for the person behind the screen?

Our product What we build. One starting point for all the apps, documents and information an employee needs. With Kelly, our digital colleague, who searches along and gives answers. That gives people more overview and calm in their working day. Discover our product →
