Care

Kelly AI

The problem: Too much admin, too little time for clients.

Kelly is our newest product, built for care. Not a chatbot, but a digital colleague who knows where everything is.

Care staff ask a question in plain language and get an answer right away. From all connected systems: protocols, HR, the knowledge base and the intranet. Tailored to their role, on any device, without training. And she helps them report faster, so they can get back to their clients.

Kelly is new, but we already know the market. You first bring her to care organisations we already work with. They know and trust us, and that is needed before they let AI into their daily work. Every improvement you ship saves care staff time. You shape what a digital colleague in care can do and test it with real care teams. You see right away what it changes in their working day.

Care

Zorgworkspace

The problem: Care work costs hours on handovers and summaries.

Care workers choose their job for people, not for logins and forms.

Zorgworkspace is built specifically for care, based on everything we have learned in this sector. It is designed around the people on the care floor. One place to start their shift, with everything they need within reach. Less searching, less admin and more time for care.

You take a product built for care to the customers who need it most. Your first big mission is moving our existing customers in care to Zorgworkspace. And making that move a real step forward for every care worker. You work with customers who already know and trust us. That means real users and fast feedback from day one. Then you go deeper and win the care organisations that still work with separate systems.

Government

Government Workspace

The problem: Too dependent on US Big Tech for data and daily work.

Governments want control over their data and their digital future.

Government Workspace gives them a safe, intelligent workspace with less dependence on Big Tech. Data stays where it belongs, and government decides for itself which tools it uses.

Governments are now really getting to work on digital sovereignty, and the market is opening up. Nobody has won it yet. You open doors and learn how government decides and buys. And you help shape what the safe government workspace of the future looks like. Every new customer makes the next one easier to win.

Other markets

Workspace 365

The problem: Scattered apps. And our base for new products.

Workspace 365 is the product where it all started. It is the foundation under everything we build.

Customers in many sectors rely on it every day to bring their apps, information and communication together. Besides government and care, this team focuses on three other sectors.

You work with a large, loyal group of customers and a product that proves itself every day. Together with your team, you choose which three sectors those are. You make sure customers stay happy and you grow with them. With AI and automation, you keep making the product better. And what we learn here, we take into everything new we build.
