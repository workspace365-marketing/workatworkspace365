Our culture

It starts with us.

Everything simplified is what we promise our customers. Three core values describe how we make that true ourselves, from day one.

Own it Build together Keep it simple

Ownership

Own it

You get trust, influence and responsibility. And you want to win.

With us, you get a challenge that is really yours. Your team sets its own priorities and plans the work. You don't wait for permission. You pick it up and finish it. If something doesn't go to plan, you take responsibility for it yourself. And you make sure it goes better next time.

Key behaviours

  • Initiative. You pick things up without waiting for permission.
  • Making choices. You set priorities and make decisions.
  • Follow-through. You finish what was agreed.
  • Accountability. You take responsibility for the result, even when things go wrong.
  • Drive to win. You go for results, with customers and as a team.

Collaboration

Build together

We make each other better. By being honest and having fun.

Whatever you do, you never do it alone. We speak to each other directly and with respect, and we ask for feedback ourselves. A solid discussion about the content gets us further than a meeting where everyone nods. And we celebrate success together.

Key behaviours

  • Asking for feedback. You ask for feedback and act on it.
  • Candour. You hold others to account on agreements and behaviour, directly and with respect.
  • Having the conversation. When things rub, you talk about it and keep it about the content.
  • Helpfulness. You share knowledge and help colleagues who are stuck.
  • Celebrating together. You build trust and fun, and celebrate success.

Simplify

Keep it simple

We remove what doesn't help and automate what we can.

We promise our customers a simple and intelligent workspace. So our own work has to be simple too. We cut steps that add nothing and leave repetitive work to AI and automation. That leaves more time for customers and for building.

Key behaviours

  • Questioning the status quo. You ask what can go, what can be smarter and what can be simpler.
  • Automating. You don't have people do repetitive work when AI can do it better.
  • Embracing change. You see change as an opportunity.
  • Experimenting. You try new things and stop what doesn't work.
  • Focus. You dare to say no to what adds little value.

Life at our office

A place where you like to be.

Our office in Nijkerk is where we meet, think and build. And where we play a game of table football, have lunch together and catch up on the sofa.
