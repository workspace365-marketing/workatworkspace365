How you see it

Own it

  • You finish what was agreed and speak up early when something gets stuck.
  • You take responsibility for the result, good or bad, and don't blame others.
  • You want to win: with customers, in the market and as a team.

Build together

Building together also means having fun together.

We know each other, spend time together, are there for each other and celebrate success. A customer won, a successful release or a colleague taking a step forward. Sometimes over drinks, sometimes just around the table with the team.
